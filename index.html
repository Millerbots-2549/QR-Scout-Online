About QR Scout Online

What is QR Scout Online? Free FRC scouting & sheet scout tool

QR Scout Online is a free FRC scouting app built for FIRST Robotics Competition teams. It lets scouts collect match scouting data on any phone, tablet, or laptop using a simple form, then sync scouting data to Google Sheets automatically. Each entry can also be saved as a QR code backup you can scan later — no internet required during the match.

Whether you need a QR scout tool, a sheet scout solution to log FRC match data into Google Sheets, or a full robot scouting app for your team, QR Scout Online works out of the box. No backend setup, no hosting, and no Google password required — you share your own sheet with our service account as an Editor.

Who is it for?

  • FRC teams that need a free scouting app for regional and championship events
  • Scouts collecting match data on paperless robot scouting forms
  • Teams wanting FRC scouting to Google Sheets for live analysis
  • Anyone who wants QR code backups of scouting entries

Key features

  • FRC match scouting form — configurable sections and fields for any game
  • Sync to Google Sheets — every commit becomes a row in your own sheet
  • QR code generator — offline, scannable scouting backups
  • Offline queue — entries sync when you're back online
  • Installable PWA & desktop apps — run it like a native FRC scouting app

Frequently asked questions

FRC scouting, QR Scout & Google Sheets FAQ

What is an FRC scouting app?

An FRC scouting app collects match performance data for FIRST Robotics Competition teams so you can analyze opponents and build alliance strategy.

How do I sync FRC scouting data to Google Sheets?

Create a Google Sheet, share it with the QR Scout service account as an Editor, enter the Sheet ID in the Scout tab, then commit entries. Each commit appends a row to your sheet.

Can I use QR Scout Online offline?

Yes. Entries are queued on the device while offline and sync automatically when the connection returns. QR codes also work fully offline as scannable backups.

Is QR Scout Online free?

Yes. QR Scout Online is a free scouting app for FRC teams. There is no charge to use the website, PWA, or desktop apps.

QR Scout Online setup

Start guide sync scouting data to Google Sheets

Want to send QR Scout match data like 2549 Millerbots scouting into your own Google Sheet without hosting anything yourself? You don't need to set up a backend or GitHub Pages. Just (1) create a Google Sheet, (2) share it with our service account so the app is allowed to write to it, and (3) enter your Sheet ID and a tab name in the app. After that, every entry you commit lands in your own spreadsheet.

Part 1: Create your Google Sheet

1

Make a new Google Sheet

Go to sheets.new (or Google Drive → New → Google Sheets) and create a blank spreadsheet to hold your scouting data.

2

Copy the Sheet ID

Look at the sheet's address bar: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/SHEET_ID/edit. Copy the long ID between /d/ and /edit - you'll paste it into the app in Part 3.

Part 2: Share it with our service account

3

Open Share

In your sheet, click the Share button in the top-right corner.

4

Add the service account email

Paste this email into the "Add people and groups" box, set its access to Editor, then click Send (you can untick "Notify people"):

(service email not set - ask the site owner)

Why this step matters

This is what lets the website write rows into your sheet. Without it the app can't add any data and you'll see a permission error when you sync.

Part 3: Enter it in the app and submit

5

Load a config

On the Home tab, click Import config to upload your own JSON, or Load sample to start from the example. This takes you to the Scout tab.

6

Save your sheet

Click Show sync, paste your Google Sheet ID, optionally set a Tab name, then click Save sheet.

7

Commit and sync

Fill out the form, click Commit, then choose Sync to Sheet. A new row appears in your own Google Sheet. You can also generate a QR code for an offline backup.

Troubleshooting

  • Permission or 403 errors - the sheet was not shared with the service account email above as an Editor.
  • Nothing happens / network error - make sure the site is open over https:// or http://, not a local file:// path.
  • Wrong sheet or wrong tab - double-check the Sheet ID (it must be the part between /d/ and /edit) and the tab name.
