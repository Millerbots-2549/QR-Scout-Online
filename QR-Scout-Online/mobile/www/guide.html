What is FRC scouting?

FRC scouting is the process of collecting performance data about other teams during FIRST Robotics Competition matches. Teams use match scouting data to evaluate alliance partners, plan elimination-round strategy, and write match descriptions. A good robot scouting program helps you win more matches.

How to scout FRC matches (step by step)

  1. Pick what to track. Common data points: auto points, game pieces scored, defense played, penalties, and end-game actions.
  2. Use a consistent form. The QR Scout Online FRC scouting app gives every scout the same fields so data stays comparable.
  3. Record every match. Scout your own team and opponents. More match data means better analysis.
  4. Save backups. Generate a QR code for each entry so you never lose data if the network drops.
  5. Sync to Google Sheets. Export all entries into your own Google Sheet for sorting, filtering, and alliance selection.

Robot scouting best practices

  • Assign one scout per team per match for consistent observations.
  • Use numeric counters and checkboxes instead of free text where possible.
  • Review data between matches to adjust alliance strategy.
  • Keep QR code backups as an offline safety net.
