Sheet Scout — Sync FRC Scouting Data to Google Sheets

Sheet Scout is the free FRC scouting to Google Sheets feature of QR Scout Online. Collect match scouting data with the FRC scouting app and every committed entry lands as a row in your own spreadsheet. No backend setup, no Google password required.

Open the FRC scouting app →

How Sheet Scout works

  1. Create a Google Sheet — open sheets.new to make a blank spreadsheet for your FRC match data.
  2. Copy the Sheet ID — it's the long string between /d/ and /edit in your sheet URL.
  3. Share with the service account — add the QR Scout service account email as an Editor so the app can write to your sheet.
  4. Enter the Sheet ID — on the Scout tab, click "Show sync," paste your Sheet ID, set a tab name, and save.
  5. Commit entries — each scouting entry appends a new row to your Google Sheet automatically.

Why use Sheet Scout for FRC?

  • Your data, your sheet — entries go to a spreadsheet you own and control.
  • No passwords shared — only the service account gets Editor access to your sheet.
  • Works offline — entries queue and sync when you're back online.
  • QR code backups — scannable offline copies of every entry.
  • Free for every FRC team — no subscription, no login.
