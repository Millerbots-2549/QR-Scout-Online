FRC Scout App · QR Scout Online

FRC Scout App — Collect Match Data & Sync to Google Sheets

QR Scout Online is a free FRC scout app for FIRST Robotics Competition teams. Collect match scouting data on any phone, tablet, or laptop, then sync scouting data to Google Sheets with one tap. It also works as a sheet scout and robot scouting tool, with QR code backups that scan offline.

How the FRC scout app works

Collect match scouting data in three steps

1

Load a scouting config

Import your own JSON config or load the built-in 2549 Millerbots sample to set up your FRC match scouting form.

2

Scout each match

Fill out the robot scouting form during the match. Generate a QR code backup for every entry so nothing is lost.

3

Sync to Google Sheets

Enter your Google Sheet ID and commit. Every entry lands as a new row in your own spreadsheet — a true sheet scout workflow.

Why teams use QR Scout Online

Free FRC scouting app features

  • FRC match scouting form — configurable sections and fields for any game
  • Sync to Google Sheets — every commit becomes a row in your own sheet
  • QR code generator — offline, scannable scouting backups
  • Offline queue — entries sync when you're back online
  • No backend setup — static site, works on any device
  • Free for all FRC teams — no login, no subscription
